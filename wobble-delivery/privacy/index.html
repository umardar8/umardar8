Privacy Policy for Wobble Delivery

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Wobble Delivery is a mobile game published by Mufasa Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how Wobble Delivery handles user data.

1. Summary

Wobble Delivery is designed as an offline game. We do not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal data from users.

2. Data We Collect

Wobble Delivery does not collect personal or sensitive user data. The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera data, device identifiers, advertising IDs, payment information, or account information.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The game may save gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, unlocked levels, best scores or medals, and game settings such as sound, music, assist mode, or reduced motion.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to Mufasa Apps or to any server.

4. Internet, Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

Wobble Delivery does not use internet-based features, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, account systems, payment systems, or social login systems.

The app does not show ads and does not track users across apps or websites.

5. Permissions

Wobble Delivery does not request Android permissions for camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, notifications, or internet access.

6. Sharing Result Cards

Wobble Delivery may generate a result card locally on your device. If you choose to share that card using your device's sharing options, the selected app or service will handle the shared content according to its own privacy policy. Mufasa Apps does not receive a copy of the shared content.

7. Children's Privacy

Wobble Delivery is a family-friendly game and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Wobble Delivery does not collect data on our servers, we do not retain user data externally.

Local game progress and settings remain on your device until you reset progress inside the game, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

9. Third-Party Services

Wobble Delivery does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, account, payment, or networking services.

10. Security

Since Wobble Delivery does not transmit personal data to our servers, there is no server-side user data storage. Local game data remains on your device and is managed by your device operating system.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Wobble Delivery changes how it handles data. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
